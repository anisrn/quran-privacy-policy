Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026  ·  Package: com.qurantarteel.app

Quran - Prayer, Qibla & Duas is built with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, sell, or share any personal information. All your data stays on your device.
📍

1. Data We Access

The app requests permission to access the following on your device:

Data Type Purpose Stored?
Device Location
(Foreground only)		 Calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your city Device only
Device Magnetometer / Compass Show real-time Qibla compass direction Not stored

Location is only requested when you open the app. It is never tracked in the background, never sent to our servers, and never shared with third parties.

🌐

2. Third-Party APIs Used

The app fetches content from the following public APIs. Only the data needed to display the requested content is sent (e.g., your coordinates for prayer times).

Service URL What is sent What is received
Aladhan api.aladhan.com Latitude, longitude, date, calculation method Prayer times, Qibla angle
Quran.com API api.quran.com Surah/ayah identifiers, language preference Arabic text, translations, chapter info
EveryAyah everyayah.com Reciter ID, surah & ayah numbers MP3 audio recitations
fitrahive/dua-dhikr raw.githubusercontent.com Category slug (e.g., morning-dhikr) Dua & Dhikr text (Arabic, transliteration, translation)
spa5k Tafsir API api.quran.com/tafsir Surah & ayah identifiers Tafsir (Quran commentary) text
dua-dhikr API dua-dhikr (GitHub) Category identifier Duas & Dhikr content in English

These are free, public Islamic services. We have no affiliation with them. Please review their own privacy policies for more details.

💾

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All preferences and cached data are stored exclusively on your device using AsyncStorage and the device file system. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Key / Location What it stores
@salahtime/quran-reading-settings Reading preferences: Arabic font, font size, translation language, reciter, etc.
@salahtime/bookmarks Quran ayah bookmarks you have saved
@qurantarteel/last_read Last read surah and ayah position
@salahtime/madhab Your selected Islamic jurisprudence school (Shafi'i, Hanafi, etc.)
@salahtime/theme-mode App theme preference (light / dark)
@salahtime/quran-chapters Offline cache of the 114 Quran chapter list
@salahtime/quran-verses-* Offline cache of verse text and translations per surah
documentDirectory/quran_dl/ Audio MP3 files you choose to download for offline listening

5. Android Permissions Explained

Permission Why it is needed
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 To determine your city for accurate prayer time calculations and Qibla direction. Only used when the app is open.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS To send you local prayer time reminder notifications (Adhan alerts). No internet is needed for this — all scheduling is done on-device.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED To reschedule prayer time alarms after the device restarts, so you never miss a prayer notification.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM To deliver prayer notifications at the exact calculated prayer time.
INTERNET To fetch Quran text, audio recitations, and prayer times from the public APIs listed above.
